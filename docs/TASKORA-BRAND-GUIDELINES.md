# TASKORA — Brand Identity & Export Kit

## 1. Core identity
Brand: TASKORA
Concept: Task → Action → Completion → Reward → Opportunity.
The symbol uses a proprietary geometric form with connected movement and a subtle negative-space T. It is designed to remain recognizable at small sizes and in monochrome.

## 2. Official master files
- public/brand/taskora-logo-horizontal-color.svg — primary horizontal logo.
- public/brand/taskora-logo-horizontal-black.svg — black version.
- public/brand/taskora-logo-horizontal-white.svg — white/reversed version.
- public/brand/taskora-symbol-color.svg — isolated symbol.
- public/brand/taskora-symbol-black.svg — isolated black symbol.
- public/brand/taskora-symbol-white.svg — isolated white symbol.
- public/brand/taskora-symbol-mono.svg — single-color master.

All SVG files are vector masters and do not depend on Lovable, icon libraries, stock images or external image assets.

## 4. Typography
Primary typeface: Inter
Wordmark weight: ExtraBold / 800
Supporting interface weights: Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700.
Secondary typeface: none required for the core identity.

The supplied SVG wordmark uses Inter, Arial, sans-serif as a resilient fallback stack. For professional reproduction, use the exact Inter font family.

## 5. Variants
- Horizontal: symbol + TASKORA wordmark.
- Compact: reduced horizontal mark.
- Isolated symbol: app icon, favicon, avatar and small spaces.
- Light background: color or black master.
- Dark background: white/reversed master.
- Monochrome: single-color master when color reproduction is unavailable.

## 6. Clear space
Use X = the diameter of the symbol circular detail as the base unit. Maintain at least 1X clear space around the complete horizontal logo. Do not place text, borders, icons, photographs or other marks inside this protected area.

## 7. Minimum size
- Horizontal digital use: 120 px wide minimum.
- Isolated symbol: 20 px minimum for digital interfaces; 12 px only for specialist favicon/system contexts after legibility testing.
- Print: 25 mm horizontal logo minimum recommended; 5 mm symbol minimum subject to production method.

## 8. Proportion and misuse
Never stretch, compress, rotate, skew, redraw, outline, add shadows, add effects, change the wordmark lettering, rearrange the symbol and wordmark arbitrarily, or place the logo on a background that reduces contrast.

## 9. Applications
The masters are intended for website, mobile app, favicon, social profiles, documents, business cards, clothing, advertising, print and corporate materials.

## 10. Export principle
SVG is the authoritative master. PNG is for raster use with transparent backgrounds. PDF is for vector print workflows. Keep the original SVG masters unchanged and derive other formats from them.
